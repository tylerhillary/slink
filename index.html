Why we built it →

Balanced by design

You name one skill to learn and the skills you can teach. We work both directions until the trade is even — not a one-way favour that quietly dies after week two.

Verified before introduced

Identity, claimed expertise and references are checked by a person, not a form. Every profile you meet has already cleared that gate.

Sessions with a shape

Each exchange starts with an agreed goal, a cadence and a finish line. You both know what "done" looks like before the first call.

Concierge matching

A person reads every registration

No algorithm decides who you meet. Our team reads what you wrote, checks what you claimed, and hand-picks the shortlist. If a match isn't working, you tell us and we re-pair you — free, and without making it awkward.

How it works

Four steps between now and your first session.

Registration takes about three minutes, and a human reads every single one.

Register now
  1. 01

    Declare the trade

    Name the one skill you want to learn and list what you can teach in return. Be specific — "React state management" beats "coding".

    ≈ 3 minutes
  2. 02

    We verify and shortlist

    Our team checks your details, then hand-picks members whose teaching skill answers your goal and whose learning goal answers yours.

    Within 24 hours
  3. 03

    Agree the terms

    Pick your match, then settle the plain stuff together: how many sessions, how often, and what you'll both have to show at the end.

    One short call
  4. 04

    Start trading

    Run your sessions. We check in at the midpoint and at the finish, and re-pair you free of charge if the fit isn't right.

    Ongoing
