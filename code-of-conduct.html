Conduct & Safety

Code of Conduct

Last Updated: 22 October 2026 Effective Date: 22 October 2026 Version: 2026-10-22

In short

The plain version of what follows. The detail below governs if the two ever differ:

  • Teach what you actually know, turn up when you said you would, and say so early if you cannot.
  • Sessions happen online. Nobody has to meet anyone in person, and no member may make it a condition.
  • Nobody at Slink360 will ever ask you to pay to be introduced, or to pay another member.
  • If something feels wrong, tell us. You do not need proof, and a report made in good faith is never held against you.
  • We can warn a member, end an exchange, remove somebody for good, or go to the police.

1. Who this applies to

Everyone who registers with Slink360, from the moment the registration is filed. It applies to what you do on the exchange and to how you behave towards another member anywhere else — on a call, over WhatsApp, by email, or in person — where that contact came about because of Slink360.

This page sits alongside our Terms of Service, which is the agreement between you and us. Where this page and the terms cover the same ground, the terms govern; this page explains what they mean in practice.

2. What we expect

An exchange is two people giving each other their time. Almost everything below follows from that:

  • Teach what you actually know. Do not claim expertise you do not have, and do not take on something you are not competent to teach. Saying “that part is beyond me” is a perfectly good answer.
  • Turn up. Agree a schedule you can keep, and keep it. If you cannot make a session, say so before it starts, not after.
  • Say it early if it is not working. A bad fit is not a failure and not a complaint — tell your partner, tell us, and we will re-pair you free of charge.
  • Give as well as take. Every exchange here runs both ways. Taking your sessions and then disappearing before you teach yours is the one thing that breaks this for everybody.
  • Treat the other person's details as theirs. Use them for the exchange and nothing else — not marketing, not recruitment, not resale, not adding anyone to a list.
  • Keep confidences. Members share work, clients and plans in sessions. If it was told to you in confidence, it stays there.
  • Ask before you record. No session is recorded, photographed or screen-captured without the other person agreeing first, and nobody else joins a session without both people knowing.

3. What is not allowed

Any of these can end your membership, in some cases without warning:

  • Harassment, intimidation, threats, or persistent contact after somebody has asked you to stop.
  • Sexual advances, sexual comments, or any sexual content in a session. An exchange is not a date and nobody here consented to be approached that way.
  • Discrimination or abuse on the basis of ethnicity, state of origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or anything like them.
  • Lying about who you are, what you can teach, or what you have done.
  • Asking another member for money in order to be introduced, matched or recommended — or claiming to speak for Slink360 when you do not.
  • Asking for passwords, bank details, card numbers, BVN, or copies of identity documents. We never need these from one member to another, and neither do you.
  • Pressuring somebody to meet in person, to move to a private channel, or to continue in a way they have said they are not comfortable with.
  • Passing on another member's contact details, messages or materials to anyone else without their permission.
  • Using the exchange to recruit for, sell, or promote anything unrelated to the skills being traded.

4. Sessions happen online

Slink360 is an online-first exchange. Sessions are expected to run over video call, voice call or chat — whatever the two of you already use. That is the default, and for a first session it is the only thing we recommend.

This is deliberate. Online, you can end a session the moment you want to, there is a record that it happened, and neither of you has told a stranger where you will be. Most of what members trade here — design, code, marketing, bookkeeping, languages — works perfectly well on a screen.

Three things follow from it:

  • You never have to meet anybody in person. Not for a first session, not ever.
  • No member may make meeting in person a condition of the exchange. If somebody tells you the trade can only happen face to face, they are asking for something we do not require — and that is worth telling us about.
  • Slink360 never arranges, requires, attends or supervises an in-person meeting. We do not vet venues and we are not there.

5. If you both choose to meet

Some skills really are easier hand to hand, and two adults may decide to meet. If you do, you do so at your own risk — see section 6.2 of the terms — and we ask you to take the ordinary precautions:

  • Meet in a public place with other people around. Never at a home, and never in a hotel room.
  • Tell somebody you trust where you are going, who you are meeting and when you expect to be back.
  • Keep the first meeting short. You are not obliged to stay, and you owe nobody an explanation for leaving.
  • Do not carry cash, and do not hand over documents.
  • Arrange it yourselves in writing, so there is a record of what was agreed.
  • Stop immediately if you feel uncomfortable, and tell us afterwards.

You are welcome to tell the desk before you meet somebody, so that we have a note of it. We will not talk you out of it; we would simply rather know.

6. Money

Registering, being verified, being matched and being introduced are free of charge, and nobody at Slink360 will ever ask you to pay for any of it. If anyone contacts you claiming to represent us and asks for payment, card details, bank details or a password, do not provide them — tell us instead. See section 9 of the terms.

If you and another member decide on a paid arrangement between yourselves, that is your own business and your own risk. We do not take a fee from it, do not process it, do not hold funds, and will not mediate a dispute about it. No member is ever required to pay another member to use Slink360.

7. How to report a member

If a member behaves in a way that worries you — harassment, pressure, dishonesty, unwanted contact, a request for money, anything that feels unsafe — tell us. You do not need evidence and you do not need to be certain. You will not be penalised for making a report in good faith, and we do not require you to confront anybody first.

Three ways, all reaching the same desk:

  • Email skillbank0@gmail.com with “Report a member” in the subject line. Best if you have screenshots or messages to attach.
  • Call +234 812 820 4201, Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 WAT.
  • WhatsApp the same number, if a call is not convenient or you would rather send a voice note.

It helps if you can tell us the member's name or the skill they were teaching, roughly when it happened, and what was said or done. If you have none of that, report it anyway.

You may also report somebody on behalf of another member who does not feel able to.

8. What happens next

  • We acknowledge it within one working day, so you know a person has it.
  • We look into it. That usually means reading the exchange record and speaking to both people separately. We will not quote you to the other member without asking you first.
  • We pause what needs pausing. If there is any question of safety, we stop the exchange while we look, rather than afterwards.
  • We tell you what we decided, within five working days of acknowledging the report, so far as we properly can. There are things about another member we cannot share, and we will say when that is the case rather than go quiet.

Reports are held in confidence. We do not publish them, and we do not tell the member who reported them unless you ask us to.

9. What we may do

Depending on what we find, we may do any of the following:

  • take no action, and tell you why;
  • warn the member, and record it against their account;
  • end the exchange and re-pair the other person free of charge;
  • remove the member from Slink360 permanently; or
  • report the matter to the police.

A member removed for conduct does not get a new account under another name. If we find one, we close it.

10. If you are in immediate danger

Contact the Nigeria Police Force on 112, or your local emergency number, first. We are a small team, we are not an emergency service, and the desk is not staffed overnight. Tell us afterwards and we will act on our side.

11. Changes to this code

If we change what is expected of members, we will update the version at the top of this page and say what changed. The version in force when something happened is the one we apply to it.

Not sure whether to report something?

Ask anyway. We would much rather hear about something that turns out to be nothing than find out later that somebody sat on it.
