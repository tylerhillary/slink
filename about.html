About Slink360

We built the exchange we wanted to use.

Slink360 is a product of Radius Technology, built in Nigeria for people who are good at something and want to be good at something else. It is deliberately small, deliberately human, and deliberately not a marketplace.

Why we exist

Talent was never the shortage.

Everywhere we looked there were people who could teach something well and people who badly needed exactly that thing. They simply never met. The existing options were job boards, cold DMs, or courses that talked at you.

So we built the missing piece: a place where you state what you can give and what you need, and someone whose job it is to care about that match actually reads it. No feed. No bidding. No noise.

How we work

Three rules we don't bend.

01

Both sides give

Every match is reciprocal. If you only want to take, this isn't the place — and if you only want to give, we'll find you something you want to learn.

02

People, not algorithms

A member of our team reads every registration and makes every introduction. It doesn't scale cheaply. That is the point.

03

Say the real thing

If a match isn't working, we want to hear it early. Re-pairing is free and expected — a bad fit is data, not a failure.

Where we've got to

A short history.

2025 · Q1

Radius Technology starts the pilot

Forty people, one spreadsheet, and a lot of phone calls. Enough matches held together that the idea was worth building properly.

2025 · Q3

Slink360 opens to the public

The registration and verification flow goes live, replacing the spreadsheet with something members can use themselves.

2025 · Q4

First university partnerships

Topfaith University and the University of Uyo begin routing students to the exchange for practical skills their curricula don't cover.

2026

Across all 36 states and the FCT

Over 180 verified members trading more than 40 distinct skills, with a median match turnaround under 24 hours.
