Cookies & Storage

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 5 September 2026 Effective Date: 5 September 2026 Version: 2026-09-05
Back to Home

1. The short version

Slink360 sets no cookies at all unless you press Accept. There are no advertising cookies, no tracking pixels, and no third-party marketing tags anywhere on this site. If you decline the banner, or simply ignore it, nothing is stored except your choice not to be tracked.

This policy explains exactly what is stored, when, and how to get rid of it. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. What we mean by “cookies”

Cookies are small text files a website places on your device. Related technologies — localStorage and sessionStorage — store information in your browser in a similar way. We treat all of them as “cookies” for the purposes of this policy, and we list every one we use below.

3. Everything this site stores

None of the first three tell us anything about you — they never leave your device and we cannot read them. Only the Google Analytics cookies send anything anywhere, and only with your agreement.

4. What we do not do

Because cookie policies are often written to describe things a site might one day do, here is what this one does not:

  • We do not run advertising cookies, retargeting, or conversion pixels. There is no Meta Pixel, no LinkedIn Insight Tag, no TikTok pixel, no Google Ads tag.
  • We do not sell or share anything cookies collect.
  • We do not build a profile of you, or track you across other websites.
  • We have no logins, so there are no authentication or session cookies.
  • We do not use cookies to make any decision about your registration or your match. A person does that.

5. Third parties

The only third party that can set a cookie here is Google, through Google Analytics, and only after you accept. Google's own handling of that data is governed by its privacy terms.

Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, and our site is delivered by Vercel. Neither sets cookies on this site, though both will see your IP address as part of delivering the page — as any web host does.

6. Changing your mind

You can withdraw consent at any time, and it is as easy to withdraw as it was to give:

  • Clear this site's data in your browser. Your stored choice disappears with it, and the banner will ask again on your next visit. In most browsers this is under Settings → Privacy → Cookies and site data.
  • Block cookies for this site in your browser settings. The site will still work; the banner simply will not be able to remember your answer.
  • Write to us at skillbank0@gmail.com and we will tell you exactly what we hold.

Declining costs you nothing. Every part of Slink360 — registering, being matched, being introduced — works identically whether you accept or decline.

7. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because we set no analytics cookies without explicit consent, a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal from your browser changes nothing about how we behave: we were already not tracking you until you said we could.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever add a new cookie, we will add it to the table in section 3 before it is set, update the version at the top, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

Questions about cookies?

Ask us. It is a short list and we are happy to walk through it.
