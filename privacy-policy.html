1. Who we are, and what this covers

Slink360 is a skill exchange operated by Radius Technology, based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Radius Technology is the data controller for the information described here, which means we are the ones responsible for it and the ones you hold to account.

This policy covers the Slink360 website, the skill registration form, our contact channels (email, telephone and WhatsApp), and the emails we send you as part of making an introduction. Together we call these the “Services”.

It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the “NDPA”), which replaced the NDPR 2019 as Nigeria's primary data protection law.

2. What we collect

2.1 What you type into the registration form

Every field on the registration form, and nothing beyond it:

  • Your full name — so we know who we are introducing.
  • Your email address — the only channel we use to send you a shortlist or an introduction.
  • Your age — to confirm you are eligible to use the exchange.
  • Your gender — used only where a member has asked to be matched with someone of the same gender, and never used to rank or filter you otherwise.
  • Your state of residence — to match you with someone in reach.
  • Your mobile number, with country code — so the desk can reach you about your match.
  • The skill you want to learn, and the skills you can teach — the substance of the match.
  • Your consent — recorded with the date, time and the version of this policy you agreed to.

We also record a registration reference (in the form SL-YYMM-XXXX) so you can quote it back to us.

2.2 What you send us directly

If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we keep what you send — including your phone number and anything you attach — so we can answer you and keep a record of the conversation.

2.3 What is collected automatically

  • Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies when the banner asks. If you decline, or ignore it, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is collected. See our Cookie Policy.
  • Server logs held by our hosting provider, which include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Browser storage on your own device — your cookie choice, and the registration reference shown on your confirmation page. This never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

2.4 What we do not collect

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, payment card numbers, bank details, passwords, identity document numbers, health information, or details of your religion, ethnicity, political views or trade union membership. We have no use for any of it. If you send such information anyway, we will delete it.

3. Why we are allowed to hold it

Under the NDPA we must have a lawful basis for processing your data. Ours are:

  • Your consent, given by ticking the box on the registration form, for holding your details in order to verify you and find you a match. You can withdraw it at any time (see section 9), and withdrawing it does not affect anything we did lawfully beforehand.
  • Performance of a contract — carrying out the introduction you asked us to make.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the exchange safe, preventing abuse, and defending ourselves against claims — balanced against your rights, and never in a way that overrides them.
  • Legal obligation, where a law, regulator or court requires us to keep or produce something.

4. How we use it

We use your information for these purposes and no others:

  • To read and verify your registration, which a person does by hand.
  • To find members whose teaching skill answers your goal, and whose goal you can answer.
  • To send you a shortlist, and to introduce you and your match to each other by email once you have chosen.
  • To contact you about your registration, by email, telephone or WhatsApp.
  • To investigate a report about conduct on the exchange, and to remove members where necessary.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which parts of the site work — only where you accepted analytics cookies.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use it to train machine learning models. We do not send marketing emails unless you have asked for them.

When we introduce you to a match, we share your name and email address with that person, and theirs with you — that is the purpose of the introduction, and it only happens after you have chosen them from a shortlist. We do not pass on your phone number, age or gender unless you tell us to.

5. The public directory

When you register, we create a second, separate record in a public directory. It contains only:

  • the skills you offer to teach;
  • the skill you want to learn;
  • your state of residence;
  • the date you joined.

It contains no name, no email address, no phone number, no age and no gender. This is enforced technically, not just as a policy: the database rules that govern that collection permit only the fields listed above, so no personal detail can be written into it even by mistake.

This directory is what powers the tutor counts and suggested matches you see on the site. If you would prefer not to appear in it at all, tell us and we will remove your entry — it does not affect your registration or your chances of a match.

6. Who else sees it

Your information is handled by the people at Radius Technology who run the exchange, and by the following service providers, who act on our instructions:

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud) — our database and application infrastructure. Our Firestore database is located in Google's africa-south1 region (Johannesburg, South Africa), so your data leaves Nigeria and is stored in South Africa.
  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting and delivery, including server logs. Vercel is based in the United States.
  • Our email provider — used to send confirmations and introductions on our behalf. This provider may process your name and email address outside Nigeria.

Where your data is transferred outside Nigeria, we rely on the recipient being in a jurisdiction with adequate protection, or on contractual safeguards with the provider, as the NDPA requires.

We will also disclose information where we are legally required to — by a court order, a regulator, or law enforcement acting under proper authority — and where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm to someone.

If Radius Technology is ever sold or merged, member information may transfer as part of that transaction. We would tell you first, and the buyer would be bound by this policy or one no less protective.

7. How we protect it

We want to describe this accurately rather than impressively:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider.
  • Registrations can be written by the website but not read by it. Your name, email, phone number and age cannot be retrieved through the website by anyone, including another member. They are readable only by our server-side systems and by the staff who administer them.
  • Access to the administrative console is limited to the small number of people at Radius Technology who need it.
  • Introductions are delivered by email rather than exposed in a browser, deliberately, so that member contact details are never loaded into a web page.

No online service can promise absolute security, and we do not. What we can say is what we have actually built, which is the above.

8. How long we keep it

  • Registrations that lead to a match: for 24 months after the exchange ends, so we can help if a dispute or a safety question arises afterwards.
  • Registrations we could not match: for 12 months, after which we delete them or strip them of anything identifying.
  • Contact and support correspondence: for 24 months.
  • Public directory entries: for as long as your registration is active, and deleted when it is.
  • Consent records: for 6 years, because they are our evidence that we were entitled to hold your data in the first place. This is the one record that survives a deletion request, and it is kept in the narrowest form possible.
  • Reports of misconduct: for 6 years, so that a pattern of behaviour can be recognised.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • Know what we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your data — the “right to be forgotten” — where we no longer need it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, which stops future processing that relied on it.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Restrict processing while a dispute about accuracy is resolved.
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Not be subject to a decision made purely by automation. This one is easy for us: no decision about you on Slink360 is automated. A person reads every registration and makes every introduction.

To exercise any of these, email skillbank0@gmail.com with “Data request” in the subject line, and tell us which right you are exercising. Quote your registration reference if you have it. We will respond within 30 days. We do not charge for this. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your data to somebody else.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle it, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), which regulates data protection in Nigeria. You do not need our permission, and you can go to them directly.

10. Young people

Slink360 is for adults aged 18 and over. The registration form will not accept an age below 18, and this is enforced both in the form and in our database rules.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has registered — whether we notice it, or you tell us — we will delete their registration and any directory entry, and we will not make an introduction. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us information, write to us and we will remove it promptly. You do not need to explain why.

11. If something goes wrong

If a breach of personal data occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will report it to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as the NDPA requires. Where the risk to you is high, we will tell you directly and without undue delay — what happened, what data was involved, what we have done about it, and what you should do.

12. If you are outside Nigeria

Slink360 is built for Nigeria and most of our members are here, but we do occasionally match members elsewhere.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you rights equivalent to those in section 9, plus the right to complain to your national supervisory authority. Our lawful bases are as set out in section 3. Because our infrastructure sits outside the EEA and UK, your data will be transferred internationally; we rely on appropriate safeguards with our providers for that.

Wherever you are, you can use the contact route in section 9 and we will apply the strongest of the applicable protections.

13. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the “Last Updated” date and the version number at the top. Where a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell registered members by email before it takes effect, and where the change requires it, we will ask for your consent again rather than assume it.

Previous versions are available on request.
