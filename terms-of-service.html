4. What “verified” means, and what it does not

We describe members as “verified”. It is important that you understand exactly what that word means here, because it is narrower than it might sound.

Verification means: a person at Radius Technology has reviewed the registration, applied reasonable care to check that the stated contact details are real and reachable, and made a judgement about whether the claimed expertise is plausible — which may include contacting a referee the member has offered.

Verification does not mean, and must not be relied on as meaning:

  • a criminal record check, police check or any form of background screening;
  • confirmation of identity against a government-issued document;
  • confirmation of any qualification, certificate, licence or employment history;
  • an assessment of a member's competence, teaching ability, or fitness to work with any particular person;
  • any warranty as to a member's character, honesty, conduct or safety.

Verification is a reasonable-efforts check on information members supply about themselves. People can and do misrepresent themselves, and no check we perform can reliably prevent that. You must exercise your own judgement about any member you are introduced to, and you remain responsible for that judgement.

You are never obliged to meet anyone in person, and no member is entitled to insist on it.
