Before you write

Answers to the usual questions.

Registering and being matched is free. Some members agree paid arrangements between themselves once they've met, but that is entirely their business and never a requirement.

Our median turnaround from a completed registration to a shortlist is under 24 hours. If your skill is unusual it can take a few days while we find the right counterpart — we'll tell you either way.

Tell us and we re-pair you, free of charge and without any awkward conversation on your side. A bad fit is useful information, not a complaint.

Most of our members are, and the registration form is built around Nigerian states. If you're elsewhere, write to us directly — we match remote pairs where the skills and time zones line up.

We store what you submit in order to match you and nothing more. We don't sell it. You can request an export or deletion at any time — the details are in our Privacy Policy.
